Build the MLM platform as a completely separate application and make ZEX the authoritative system for subscription activation, expiry, renewal, cancellation, and refunds.
The safest commercial structure is:
The MLM company acts as an authorized ZEX sales agent.
The customer starts the purchase on the MLM website.
Payment is collected in ZEX’s merchant account through a ZEX-created checkout session.
ZEX issues the customer invoice and activates the subscription.
ZEX records the sale against the MLM company and originating MLM seller.
ZEX periodically pays the contracted commission to the MLM company.
The MLM company distributes the six-level commissions from its own commission pool.
This avoids the two platforms independently deciding whether a subscription has been paid for.
Important compliance condition
The compensation system must reward completed retail sales—not enrollment or recruitment. The customer should not receive any MLM benefit merely because another person joins underneath them.
Under India’s Consumer Protection (Direct Selling) Rules, a direct-selling entity cannot be a pyramid or money-circulation scheme, and participant agreements, grievance handling, cooling-off/refund processes, records, and other controls are required. Have an Indian direct-selling lawyer and tax adviser approve the final compensation plan, agreements, invoices, GST treatment, and marketing claims before development or launch. See the official Consumer Protection rules page and Direct Selling Rules, 2021.
A purchaser may be inserted into the referral tree immediately, but should become an active seller only after:
Explicitly accepting the seller agreement.
Completing required KYC and tax details.
Accepting the compensation, refund, and conduct policies.
Passing account and fraud checks.
There should be no joining fee or mandatory purchase whose real purpose is eligibility to earn.

Commission rules to freeze before development
Commission is created only after confirmed payment and ZEX activation.
It remains pending during the refund/cooling-off period.
It becomes available only after that period expires.
Refunds and chargebacks reverse the corresponding commission.
A seller cannot earn from their own purchase unless specifically approved as a genuine retail-sale incentive.
If fewer than six ancestors exist, the missing commission should go to a predefined company reserve—not automatically jump to another person.
The sponsor of a member should normally be immutable.
Any exceptional sponsor correction requires approval and a permanent audit record.
Renewals need a separately documented rule: same six-level payout, reduced payout, or no payout.
Referral-tree model
Use a unilevel sponsor tree rather than a binary or forced-matrix tree.
Every account has at most one sponsor:
Seller A
├── Customer/Seller B
│   ├── Customer C
│   └── Customer/Seller D
└── Customer/Seller E
If D later sells to F, F becomes D’s direct child. For F’s purchase, the commission chain starts at D and walks upward for a maximum of six eligible sellers.
“Bottom line” should mean “a new child under the referring seller.” The system should not automatically place customers under unrelated members, because spillover placement complicates consent, auditability, and compliance.
Maintain two separate statuses:
customer_status: registered, active subscriber, expired, cancelled.
seller_status: not_enrolled, pending_KYC, active, suspended, terminated.
Thus every seller can be a customer, but every customer does not automatically become a seller.

Recommended purchase flow
Customer opens a seller’s referral URL, such as mlm.example/join/ABC123.
MLM validates the referral code and stores it in a secure session.
Customer enters or verifies the mobile number/email used for ZEX.
MLM calls ZEX to find or create a linked ZEX customer.
MLM freezes the sponsor chain for that order.
MLM calls ZEX to create a ₹3,000 checkout session.
The payment gateway collects payment into the designated merchant account.
ZEX verifies the gateway webhook.
ZEX activates or renews the subscription.
ZEX sends a signed subscription.activated webhook to MLM.
MLM records the sale and creates six pending commission entries.
After the refund-hold period, pending entries become available.
Eligible sellers request payout or receive it on a scheduled cycle.
ZEX and MLM run daily reconciliation.
The browser’s “payment successful” redirect must never activate a subscription or create commission. Only a verified server-to-server confirmation should do so.

Security requirements
OAuth 2.0 client credentials or signed service credentials between platforms.
Prefer mutual TLS plus HMAC-signed webhooks.
Separate production and staging credentials.
Encrypt PAN, bank, and identity data.
Tokenize bank information through the payout provider where possible.
MFA for administrators and payout approvers.
Role-based permissions and maker-checker approval for payouts.
Rate limiting, bot protection, device/IP risk checks, and referral-abuse detection.
No sharing of ZEX passwords with the MLM platform.
Daily encrypted backups and tested recovery procedures.
Centralized logs and alerts for failed activation, mismatched payments, webhook backlog, and abnormal commission activity.
Fraud controls
Detect and hold transactions involving:
Repeated purchases using the same device, card, bank account, PAN, or phone.
Circular or self-referral patterns.
Large bursts of accounts under one seller.
Multiple identities using the same payout account.
Refunds immediately after commission availability.
Sponsor changes around high-value transactions.
Sellers promising guaranteed income.
Purchases made only to unlock earning eligibility.
Suspicious commission should be placed in held status rather than deleted.
Reconciliation
Run three automated reconciliations daily:
Payment reconciliation: gateway payment versus ZEX order.
Subscription reconciliation: paid ZEX order versus active entitlement.
Commission reconciliation: eligible ZEX sale versus MLM commission ledger.
Every discrepancy should enter an exception queue. Neither system should silently overwrite the other.
A monthly settlement statement should include:
Gross successful sales
- Refunds
- Chargebacks
= Net eligible sales
× Contracted MLM-company commission
- Applicable deductions
= Amount payable to MLM company

Minimum launch acceptance criteria
Do not launch until the following tests pass:
A repeated API call cannot create duplicate subscriptions.
A duplicated webhook cannot create duplicate commission.
The six payouts always total exactly ₹400 for a fully eligible chain.
A refund reverses the correct six original entries.
Sponsor changes cannot rewrite historical orders.
A customer can purchase without being forced to become a seller.
A seller cannot receive payout before agreement, KYC, and hold-period completion.
MLM and ZEX reports reconcile to the payment gateway.
Every manual financial change identifies the administrator, reason, and previous value.
ZEX access continues safely if the MLM platform is temporarily unavailable.
The first deliverables should be the compensation-rule specification, identity-linking design, OpenAPI contract, webhook contract, database model, and a clickable purchase-flow prototype. These documents will remove the largest ambiguities before engineering begins.
